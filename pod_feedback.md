# Pod Feedback — Oil & Gas

## Assignment Structure

Assigning each pod a different domain dataset (Oil & Gas / Healthcare / Retail) while keeping the same diagnostic framework (profiling → anomaly detection → root-cause → visualization) worked well — it meant pods could compare notes on *technique* even though the underlying data and story were completely different. The Oil & Gas dataset in particular had a clean, discoverable root cause (a single machine's maintenance lapse), which made it a good vehicle for practicing the full workflow without the signal being buried in noise.
